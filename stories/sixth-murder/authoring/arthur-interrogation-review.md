# Arthur’s interrogation — the candlestick slip

Current playable version. The example below follows the earlier denial of carrying the trunk. The alternative luggage account is preserved in the [complete dialogue](dialogue-script.md).

Hale stays with Arthur at the front and keeps the recovery private. A separate constable guards the yard and protects Baines while she speaks in her own room; another officer checks the lodging address. Neither speaks to Arthur. Hale later escorts Arthur to the station and stays with him throughout the lodging search. Alden establishes fatal blunt trauma and post-mortem knife wounds. Hale holds the courtyard knife. There is no sheath, boot or clothing-match puzzle.

**Arthur:** How much longer are you keeping me here?

## Maggie’s account of the marriage

**Investigator:** We’ve spoken to Maggie Shaw. She says you were Nora’s husband.

**Arthur:** Who is Maggie Shaw?

**Investigator:** She worked with Nora.

**Arthur:** And why would she say that?

**Investigator:** She says she attended your wedding.

**Arthur:** I don’t know what Nora’s been telling people.

## Maggie’s account of the marriage

**Investigator:** Maggie kept this photograph of your wedding. Is that you beside Nora?

**Arthur:** Let me see it.

**Investigator:** Nora and her mother. You’re on the right.

**Arthur:** All right. We were married.

**Investigator:** Why tell us she was your sister?

**Arthur:** Because the moment I said husband, you’d stop looking for anyone else.

## The man with the trunk

**Investigator:** Mrs Baines saw a man carrying Nora’s trunk down the steps.

**Arthur:** And she didn’t see his face. You told me that.

**Investigator:** Did you see anyone else near Nora’s room?

**Arthur:** No. I went to see her, and then I left.

**Investigator:** There was blood inside the trunk.

**Arthur:** I didn’t carry it. I don’t know what else you want me to say.

## What he burned

**Investigator:** You burnt clothing and a newspaper when you got home.

**Arthur:** I was clearing some things out.

**Investigator:** That night?

**Arthur:** I’d been meaning to do it.

**Investigator:** Was there something wrong with the clothes?

**Arthur:** They were worn out. That’s why I got rid of them.

## How he found Nora

**Investigator:** How did you know Nora was staying at the Lantern?

**Arthur:** I waited outside the workshop. Followed her when she left.

**Investigator:** Did she know you were following her?

**Arthur:** She wouldn’t have spoken to me if she had.

**Investigator:** When did you learn about Bristol?

**Arthur:** When I went to her room. She said she was leaving in the morning.

## Why he went to see Nora

**Investigator:** Had Nora asked you to come and see her?

**Arthur:** I didn’t think I needed an invitation to speak to my wife.

**Investigator:** What did you want to say to her?

**Arthur:** That she could come home. We could sort it out between ourselves.

**Investigator:** What did she say?

**Arthur:** She’d made up her mind. Bristol, her sister, this job. She wouldn’t listen to reason.

**Investigator:** She told us she wanted the work.

**Arthur:** There was work here. She was throwing our marriage away.

**Investigator:** Did she ask you to leave?

**Arthur:** Yes. I wanted her to hear me out.

## His account of leaving

**Investigator:** What happened when Nora asked you to leave?

**Arthur:** We argued. Then I went.

**Investigator:** Was she hurt when you left?

**Arthur:** No. She was packing.

**Investigator:** Mrs Baines heard a cry and a thud.

**Arthur:** When?

**Investigator:** During the night.

**Arthur:** Then it could have been after I’d gone.

## The recovered candlestick

**Investigator:** We’ve recovered the candlestick Mrs Baines lent Nora.

**Arthur:** Then why isn’t George sitting here?

**Investigator:** Why George?

**Arthur:** It was under the sacks in his cart. You ought to be asking him.

**Case Map pause:** Connect Arthur’s volunteered hiding place with the candlestick recovery. The detective has not disclosed the sacks or cart in this exchange.

## Where he heard about the recovery

**Investigator:** Who told you where we found the candlestick?

**Arthur:** One of your men.

**Investigator:** Which man?

**Arthur:** I don’t remember. There were enough of you about.

**Investigator:** Did someone speak to you, or did you overhear it?

**Arthur:** Someone told me. I wasn’t taking names.

**Check his excuse:** End the exchange with Arthur and speak to Hale at the station.

## Check Arthur’s claimed source

**Investigator:** Arthur says one of our men told him where the candlestick was found. Who has spoken to him?

**Hale:** Only me, apart from you. I spoke to him at the front and brought him here myself.

**Investigator:** Did you mention the candlestick?

**Hale:** No, sir. I told him Nora had been found in the yard. Nothing about the cart search.

**Investigator:** Could he have watched it, or heard us questioning Moss and Mrs Baines?

**Hale:** No. I kept the rear passage shut. Moss spoke in the yard; an officer took him round to his horse and back without passing through the front. Mrs Baines spoke in her own room. Neither has been in to see him.

**Investigator:** And the other officers?

**Hale:** The removal men left before he arrived. The yard constable and the officer checking his address kept away from him. He’s been with me throughout. Nobody else has spoken to him.

**Case Map pause:** Connect Arthur’s claimed officer source with Hale’s account before returning to challenge him.

## Hale has checked his claim

**Investigator:** Hale was the only constable who spoke to you. He says he didn’t mention the candlestick.

**Arthur:** Then he’s forgotten.

**Investigator:** This is where we found it. Hale kept you away from the search, and the recovery wasn’t discussed in front of you.

**Arthur:** I didn’t put it there.

**Investigator:** You knew where it was before we told you.

**Arthur:** I don’t have to remember every word everyone’s said to me today.

## What actually killed Nora

**Investigator:** Hale recovered a knife beside Nora. Alden says it wasn’t what killed her.

**Arthur:** What did, then?

**Investigator:** The blow to her head. The knife wounds were made after she was dead.

**Arthur:** And you’re saying that was me?

**Investigator:** You argued with her. Baines heard a cry and a thud. You knew where the candlestick was hidden.

**Arthur:** She didn’t see me hurt Nora. Nobody did.

**Investigator:** Did you strike her?

**Arthur:** No.

## The newspaper illustration

**Investigator:** Had you read the newspaper you burnt?

**Arthur:** Of course. Everyone’s reading about the murders.

**Investigator:** Why burn it that night?

**Arthur:** I’d finished with it.

**Investigator:** The illustration shows a woman in a yard with a scarf over her face. Nora was found like that.

**Arthur:** Then perhaps you ought to be looking for the man in the papers.

**Investigator:** The wounds were made after her death. Someone wanted them to be seen.

**Arthur:** I can’t tell you why.

**Optional continuation:** The case is now reportable with his denial on record. These remaining exchanges pursue limited admissions.

## Return to the argument

**Investigator:** You said Nora was still packing when you left.

**Arthur:** She wouldn’t stop to listen to me.

**Investigator:** There was blood beneath her rug. Baines heard the disturbance. Tell us what happened when Nora wouldn’t listen.

**Arthur:** I took her arm. Just to stop her for a moment.

**Investigator:** Did she try to get away?

**Arthur:** She pulled back and fell against the washstand.

**Investigator:** You said she was packing when you left.

**Arthur:** I didn’t mean for her to fall. She pulled so hard.

**Investigator:** Did you get anyone?

**Arthur:** I thought she was dead. I didn’t know what to do.

## From the room to the yard

**Investigator:** You say Nora fell in her room. She was found outside. What happened in between?

**Arthur:** I don’t want to go through it again.

**Investigator:** Her bedding was inside the trunk, stained with blood. The packing was left in her room.

**Arthur:** I couldn’t leave her on the floor.

**Investigator:** So you put her in the trunk?

**Arthur:** I was frightened. I thought if I got her out of there, I’d have time to think.

**Investigator:** You took her into the yard.

**Arthur:** Yes.

**Investigator:** And the scarf?

**Arthur:** I covered her face. I couldn’t bear to look at her.

## Why he covered her face

**Investigator:** You say you covered her face because you couldn’t bear to look at her.

**Arthur:** She was my wife. I didn’t want people staring at her.

**Investigator:** This is how the paper shows it. You’d read it, and then you left Nora like this in the yard.

**Arthur:** They were going to blame me. You would have. Her husband, after an argument.

**Investigator:** So you wanted us to think it was him?

**Arthur:** I thought you might. If she was out there, like the woman in the paper.

**Investigator:** And the knife?

**Arthur:** I’m not saying anything about that.

**Investigator:** Alden says the blow killed her. Did you strike her with the candlestick?

**Arthur:** She fell.

**Investigator:** You still haven’t explained how you knew where it was hidden.

**Arthur:** I didn’t kill her.

The fullest account ends here. Arthur’s slip exposes knowledge; he never explicitly admits the fatal blow. The final report still requires both players’ agreement. Earlier denials and all obtained statements remain on the Case Map.
