# Historical reading list

Compare testimony, dates and interpretations before using a historical detail in a plot. These links are research references; their presence does not verify every claim on a source page.

## Start here

- [London Museum: Jack the Ripper](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/jack-ripper/) — a short overview of the murders, the women’s lives and the East End setting.

- [Hallie Rubenhold: The Five](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/434594/the-five-by-rubenhold-hallie/9781784162344) — a modern account centred on the five women. Useful for biography and daily life; compare disputed interpretations with the contemporary records.

- [Stewart P. Evans and Keith Skinner: The Ultimate Jack the Ripper Sourcebook](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/keith-skinner-4/the-ultimate-jack-the-ripper-sourcebook/9781472107855/) — a collected sourcebook of investigation material, useful beside the online inquest reports.

- [Philip Sugden: The Complete History of Jack the Ripper](https://www.littlebrown.co.uk/titles/philip-sugden/the-complete-history-of-jack-the-ripper/9781841193977/) — a detailed modern history to compare with the records and other historians’ interpretations.

## Read the murders case by case

These Casebook collections reproduce contemporary newspaper coverage of the inquests. They are valuable primary-source material, but a newspaper’s account should not automatically be treated as a complete, verbatim official deposition. Compare reports when timing or wording matters.

| Case | Source | Research focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mary Ann Nichols — 31 August | [Nichols inquest reports](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/inquests/inquest_nichols/) | Cross/Lechmere, Paul and Mizen; discovery sequence; identification; lodging and work. |
| Annie Chapman — 8 September | [Chapman inquest reports](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/inquests/inquest_chapman/) | The house and yard; Long and Cadosch; Richardson; the medical estimate and uncertain times. |
| Elizabeth Stride — 30 September | [Stride inquest reports](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/inquests/inquest_stride/) | The club, yard and discovery; neighbours and last sightings. Read Schwartz’s police account separately from the inquest coverage. |
| Catherine Eddowes — 30 September | [Eddowes inquest reports](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/inquests/inquest_eddowes/) | City custody; Watkins’s patrol; witnesses near Church Passage; identification and possessions. |
| Mary Jane Kelly — 9 November | [Kelly inquest reports](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/inquests/inquest_kelly/) | Miller’s Court; Barnett, Cox and Prater; the discovery and room access. Keep later witness statements distinct from inquest testimony. |

The [Daily Telegraph of 4 September 1888](https://www.casebook.org/press_reports/daily_telegraph/dt880904/) provides a detailed account of the Nichols discovery testimony.

## Police papers, letters and neighbourhood

- [The National Archives: Jack the Ripper source pack (PDF)](https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/education/jacktheripper.pdf) — reproductions of police and Home Office documents, with questions and contextual notes. Check each document’s date and author.

- [The National Archives: Hoax letter signed by “Jack the Ripper”](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/stories/hoax-letter-signed-by-jack-the-ripper/) — archival context for the volume of purported killer correspondence.

- [LSE: Charles Booth’s poverty maps](https://booth.lse.ac.uk/learn-more/what-were-the-poverty-maps) — useful for streets, housing and social geography. The first East End map appeared in 1889; the digitized maps also include later survey work. Check dates, and read the investigators’ classifications as the views of their period.

## Comparing accounts and public discussion

- [Chapman inquest coverage, Daily Telegraph, 13 September 1888](https://www.casebook.org/press_reports/daily_telegraph/dt880913/) — testimony about the apron and Pizer’s whereabouts.
- [Kelly inquest coverage, Morning Advertiser, 13 November 1888](https://www.casebook.org/press_reports/morning_advertiser/18881113/) — neighbours’ observations, discovery and entry into the room.
- [Warren’s report, 6 November 1888](https://www.casebook.org/official_documents/warrenlt/) — his explanation for removing the Goulston Street writing.
- [The Times, 1 October 1888](https://www.casebook.org/press_reports/times/18881001/) — contemporary reporting of the Stride and Eddowes deaths.
- [Saucy Jacky postcard: archive image and transcription](https://umsi580.lsait.lsa.umich.edu/s/jack-the-ripper-archive/item/1109) and [transcription reference](https://www.casebook.org/ripper_letters/saucy_jacky_postcard/) — compare the message, postmark and receipt date with what newspapers had published.

Keep a present-day narrator’s hindsight separate from what a person could know in 1888.
